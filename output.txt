We present a residual learning framework to ease the training of networks that are substantially deeper than those used previously . We explicitly reformulate the layers as learning residual functions with reference to the layer inputs, instead of learning unreferenced functions . We provide comprehensive empirical evidence showing that these residual networks are easier to optimize, and can gain accuracy from considerably increased depth . Deeper networks have higher training error, and thus test error . Vanishing/exploding gradients hamper convergence from the beginning .
